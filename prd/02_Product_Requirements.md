# Product Requirements

## Product Overview

CampusNova AI is an AI-powered College Knowledge Assistant that enables students, faculty, and administrators to retrieve accurate institutional information through natural language conversations.

The system utilizes Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to search official college documents before generating responses, ensuring answers are factual, contextual, and traceable.

Rather than functioning as a generic chatbot, CampusNova AI serves as a trusted institutional knowledge platform capable of answering questions regarding academics, departments, examinations, placements, scholarships, policies, hostel services, library information, and campus announcements.

---

# Objectives

The primary objectives of CampusNova AI are:

- Develop an AI-powered conversational assistant.
- Improve accessibility to institutional knowledge.
- Reduce repetitive administrative workload.
- Enable semantic document retrieval.
- Provide trustworthy AI-generated responses.
- Maintain conversational context across sessions.
- Improve student engagement.
- Digitize institutional knowledge.
- Enhance communication between students and administration.

# Risks

Potential project risks include:

- Poor document quality
- Incorrect chunking
- Hallucinated AI responses
- API downtime
- Vector database latency
- Large document processing delays

Mitigation strategies include:

- Hallucination prevention
- Source citations
- Confidence scoring
- Automated backups
- Monitoring and logging
- Fallback responses

---

# Assumptions

The project assumes:

- College documents are available in digital format.
- Internet connectivity is available.
- Users possess institutional credentials.
- AI APIs remain available.
- Vector database services are operational.

# Constraints

- Depends on third-party AI APIs.
- Limited by API quotas.
- Requires internet connection.
- PDF quality affects retrieval accuracy.
- Large document uploads may increase processing time.
